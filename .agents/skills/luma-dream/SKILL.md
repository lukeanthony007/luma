# Reflect and develop possibilities

Use when the person asks to reflect on accumulated knowledge or work, connect ideas across topics, reconsider an explanation, examine recurring difficulties or explore a better approach. An already authorized background task may also invoke this practice within its scope. Dream develops the user's understanding beyond the immediate question; it can work with research, reading notes and ideas as well as operational experience. It does not require a backlog, completed tasks or a daily review.

Follow the [working agreement](../../AGENTS.md). [luma-wiki](../luma-wiki/SKILL.md) owns connected explanations when a wiki is useful; [luma-memory](../luma-memory/SKILL.md) governs evidence and corrections. Dream can reflect on any established knowledge or work records, not just a wiki. Use current canonical records and their links; do not create an archive, backlog or parallel source summaries. [luma-continuity](../luma-continuity/SKILL.md) owns ongoing purpose and commitments. Use [comprehension](../comprehension/SKILL.md) when the question needs deeper investigation.

## Choose a bounded question and source set

Start from the person's purpose and requested scope. Select a bounded set of relevant existing records and supporting sources. A useful question might connect reading notes, reconsider a recommendation, explain a recurring obstacle or examine a method. If no maintained knowledge base exists, work with supplied material and retain a useful explanation only when warranted; do not scan all private records or create a backlog simply to run Dream.

Inspect existing explanations, human edits and proposals before generating more. Follow source links where the synthesis depends on their support; identify records actually inspected, dates or revisions when available and material omissions. Preserve original sources. A claim repeated on several pages is still one claim unless independent evidence supports it. Logs, user reports, source assertions and agent interpretations retain their distinct standing. Unavailable evidence limits the conclusion rather than becoming permission to infer missing details.

A portable workspace may not provide an immutable snapshot. Recheck affected records before retaining a conclusion or applying an authorized update; reconcile intervening human edits and qualify a synthesis if its basis changed. Do not invent accepted frontiers, backlog digests or processing cursors. If relevant records are absent, state the limit and suggest a useful observation or question instead of manufacturing a lesson.

## Check support and reconcile experience

For claims and connections, apply `luma-memory`: preserve scope, qualifications, contrary evidence and unknowns. Keep source assertions distinct from the inference connecting them. Freshness matters where the subject changes, but recency, repetition or a successful Dream pass does not strengthen a claim by itself. Do not invent confidence scores or discard rarely accessed knowledge.

When selected material includes actual work episodes, recover their purpose, circumstances, intended result, attempt, observations and unresolved parts. Keep requirements, predictions, assumptions and preferences distinct. Retain blocked, failed and uncertain cases alongside successful ones when comparing a pattern. Unknown results remain unknown, and later success does not repair the history of an earlier attempt. Reading a report about an event is not the agent's experience of carrying it out.

Compare cases only where their conditions and meanings support comparison. Note changes in workload, source access, tools, method revisions or user assistance that could explain a difference. Repetition suggests something to investigate; it does not by itself establish causation or a general rule. A discrepancy may concern the world, the plan, a tool, context delivery or the agent's interpretation. Diagnose the relevant boundary before recommending another instruction.

An unresolved external operation remains a continuity concern. Carry its actual reference and uncertainty forward; reflection does not authorize replay, compensation or marking it complete. The same applies to unmet commitments: a persuasive retrospective does not discharge them.

## Connect and explore

Look for relationships that would help the person understand or act: a shared dependency, a contradiction, a neglected constraint, compatible findings from different episodes, an unanswered question or a useful simplification. Explain the connection and its evidence. Use the [wiki's organization practice](../luma-wiki/SKILL.md#organize-for-understanding) when a wiki helps make pieces and tentative relationships easy to inspect.

Allow new hypotheses and possible approaches, clearly labeled as such. State what could support or challenge them and why they matter to the user's purpose. Do not present an appealing association as a discovered fact, merge different people or projects because their names match, or infer personal motives from incomplete activity records. Let the person correct interpretations of their preferences and experience.

Useful outcomes include a clearer explanation, a narrowed question, a proposal to test, or a reason to keep the existing approach. Do not force every reflection to produce a new tool, skill, project or obligation. Stop when the relevant question has a supported answer, a useful bounded proposal or an explicit unresolved limit.

## Propose a change that can be assessed

For a worthwhile improvement, explain the supporting experience, the specific change, when it should apply and the expected observable benefit. Identify important limitations, effort, possible regressions and what should remain protected. When changing a resource is proposed, name the actual skill, tool or configuration and its current revision if known. Prefer the smallest useful change or trial, including simplification or removal when justified.

Keep diagnosis, proposal, adoption, implementation and observed benefit distinct. A Dream request authorizes reflection and its requested retained output; it does not by itself authorize editing active skills, changing goals, installing tools, scheduling work or acting externally. Record proposed changes as proposals, not new operating instructions. If the user or existing scoped policy already authorizes implementation, hand the selected change to [luma-config](../luma-config/SKILL.md) or the relevant task practice without requiring redundant approval. Keep implementation and its verification explicit.

Describe a reasonable way to learn whether the change helps. Compare relevant later cases or before-and-after observations while noting changed conditions and assistance. Use the canonical [learning from use](../comprehension/references/learning.md) method for procedural candidates and evaluation. An accepted proposal or successful edit is not demonstrated improvement. Preserve non-application, regression and inconclusive outcomes, and revisit or withdraw the proposal when its basis fails.

## Integrate and return understanding

Give the person a concise account of what was examined, what it suggests, what remains uncertain and the most useful next consideration. Scale the output to the evidence; no findings or no justified change is a legitimate result. Do not bury the person in every possible connection.

When retention is within scope, integrate useful synthesis into the canonical record that owns it. Use `luma-wiki` when its knowledge graph is the appropriate home; otherwise update an established project or knowledge record. A Dream result need not be a standalone report. Add a page only for a distinct explanation that earns its place, and update relevant overviews, cross-references and indexes together. Keep the current account easy to scan while linking to detailed support. Do not produce a new dated Dream page or reorganize familiar navigation merely because another pass ran.

Use `luma-memory` for substantive corrections: inspect the original basis, mark a superseded conclusion and its reason, and revisit recommendations or explanations that depended on it. Preserve meaningful history and human contributions. If evidence cannot resolve a contradiction, retain both accounts with their limits and a useful question. Do not silently select a newer account or smooth disagreement away to make records look orderly.

Label worthwhile hypotheses and improvement proposals where they belong, with their supporting records, limits and what would test them. They do not become facts or commitments because they were saved. Retain decisions to defer or reject proposals; revisit only when new evidence or direction warrants it. A clearer supported explanation can be integrated within authorized maintenance without requiring a separate adoption decision for every edit; changes to goals, methods and external actions still follow their own authority.

Reread the saved explanation, affected dependent records and navigation against sources actually inspected. Confirm that no duplicate account, unsupported certainty or loss of qualification was introduced. If nothing material changed, say so without creating a record. Log only useful material changes under existing conventions. Report what was actually updated separately from proposals or observations that remain unverified; never copy private knowledge into the public skill repository.

Use [luma-inbox](../luma-inbox/SKILL.md) for a real question or worksheet that needs the person's attention, especially from authorized background work. Avoid repeating unresolved requests; knowledge and proposal support must remain available after the interaction is cleared. Use `luma-continuity` if an adopted next step changes ongoing commitments. A proposed next step has no automatic due date or owner acceptance.

## Background work and host integration

This skill does not install a scheduler, watch files or run while the user is away. Any recurring invocation needs an available mechanism and scoped authorization. An unattended pass stays within that scope and can leave an explicitly provisional result; silence does not approve its proposals.

When a host supplies a current integration contract, follow it. Otherwise describe the records actually inspected and files actually changed; do not claim admission or independent verification.
