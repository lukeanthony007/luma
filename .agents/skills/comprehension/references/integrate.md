# Integrate findings and corrections

Revise the understanding a later reader receives, within original source scope and bounds. Read current wording and relevant complete passages. Identify what remains valid, changes, is withdrawn or cannot be settled. Keep events distinct from their documentation; a correction to a report is not necessarily a new observation or physical event.

Incorporate each material finding, contest with evidence, or retain unresolved. Do not invent collaborators. Before deriving an explanation, retain source-reported inputs with their actual support: a rule or definition does not supply a particular reported value. Keep units, subject and temporal scope, material conditions and relationships. Preserve known inputs even when a result is unavailable; distinguish explicit absence, omitted value and unexamined passage. “Not verified” does not entail nonoccurrence.

Use the owning revision process. Reuse current assertions only after checking actual wording and support. Update affected conclusions and dependent explanations; historical versions do not substitute for current meaning. Check in both directions:

- **Source to saved meaning:** are required values, relationships, conditions, exceptions and unknowns expressed in current assertions?
- **Saved meaning to support:** does each substantive clause follow from its exact source passages, pinned premise assertions or explicit assumptions?

Read actual declared premises; do not borrow facts from a quotation, rationale, another assertion not declared as a premise, or memory. Inspect source context as well as references. Follow effects beyond the edited sentence, including conclusions that still depend on superseded premises. Check current consistency, but do not mistake consistency for semantic correctness.

Assess exact current wording and basis, not intended repair. Explain what changed, what remains unsupported or missing, and what was inspected. Repair demonstrated gaps within bounds and reread successors. If a distinction or premise remains unchecked, report it unresolved or unsatisfied. A self-check is not independent review. Return the revised explanation, source basis, material before/after changes and uncertainty.
