# Comprehension practice

Use these principles for source interpretation and inquiry. They do not authorize external collection, background work, or operational side effects.

## Scope and evidence

Name approved sources and revisions, intended use, current understanding, uncertainty and bounds. Preserve the requested outcome. Distinguish requirements (what must be established), predictions (what may prove wrong), assumptions (premises to examine), and preferences (desirable but not required). A citation is not evidence that its target was inspected; a closed corpus does not become larger through references.

Use the smallest method that addresses the gap. Ordinary reading needs no invented controversy, metric, staged workflow or specialist. A requested collaborator or independent review is still required; if unavailable, say so. Unresolved findings satisfy a contract only when it permits them.

## Preserve source meaning and support

Keep attribution, subject, identity, time, population, quantities, modality, conditions, exceptions and uncertainty when they affect meaning. Separate what a source says from inference and outside knowledge. Keep contradictions faithfully represented when evidence cannot resolve them. Distinguish source silence, unexamined scope, incomplete observation, inaccessible evidence and actual contradiction. “Not reported” or “not verified” is not “did not happen”; repeated reports from one origin are not independent corroboration.

Retained assertions must themselves express substantive facts and relationships. Quotations, rationales, topic inventories, nearby assertions and final answers cannot fill an assertion's missing meaning or declared premises. Save source-reported inputs before deriving outcomes; a definition alone does not support a particular reported value or event. Expose assumptions and their limits. A quotation supports only the passage it contains; necessary context and exact source location matter.

## Collaboration, when consequential

Give each participant a bounded question, source scope, relevant current understanding, method, result contract and budget. Participants need compatible references and clear responsibilities, not identical contexts. A finding carries exact support, qualifications, counterevidence and proposed effect. The receiver incorporates it, contests it with reasons, or leaves it unresolved. Sender completion is not integration.

An independent critic examines the actual current explanation and sources in both directions: omitted source meaning and unsupported added meaning. Agreement, generic advice or source coverage is not review of final wording. Reinspect changed assertions and support. Fulfilled contribution is not proof of complete adequacy; identify final meaning that remains unreviewed.

## Evaluate without overclaiming

Keep source fidelity, explanatory connection, coverage, distinctions, transfer and acknowledged limits inspectable. Do not manufacture a universal score. Reading coverage is evidence of attention, not understanding. Distinguish a changed explanation, prediction miss, unsatisfied requirement and insufficient observation. Candidate practice changes are not evidence of improvement; later use must be observed, including non-application, regressions and inconclusive outcomes.
