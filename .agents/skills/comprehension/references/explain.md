# Explain connected meaning

Use when observations or claims remain fragmented. Establish subject, source revisions, intended scope, current understanding and distinct requested outcomes. Retain a first useful partial account when evidence supports it; update it as reading adds facts, qualifications and relations. Keep unexamined scope and unanswered questions visible. For each requested outcome, identify current assertions that answer it and any missing decision or qualification. If a consequential gap is answerable within bounds, inspect the relevant source, revise the account and inspect what was actually saved. A partial account is not a stop condition while answerable required gaps remain.

Choose a representation suited to material: premises and objections, mechanism and dependencies, chronology, comparison, or a combination. Connect what enables, constrains, explains or qualifies each assertion. Keep identities, times, populations, modality and causality distinct where they affect conclusions. Distinguish direct source assertions from inference; pin actual source-backed inputs as premises before deriving results. Rationale is not a substitute for premises. Retain assumptions and counterevidence; do not force unresolved conflict into agreement.

Check recommendations against their operating conditions, versions, prerequisites and exceptions. A method for one setting does not establish safety in another. Preserve the inputs and outcomes of examples, and the variable roles, assumptions, relationships and limits of models; a label or claim that an example exists is not its explanation. Keep source regions outside the strongest example visible. See [preservation](preservation.md) for region-by-region checks and [checked conditions](checked-conditions.md) for explicit formal rules.

Return the actual explanation, supporting source selections, examined scope, assumptions, important relationships, concrete omissions and unresolved questions. Assess whether it connects and preserves meaning, not its length or representation size.
