# Apply and check understanding

## Set the test

Pin source/account revision, scope, allowed evidence access, evaluator and effort bounds. Recover criteria from the actual question or task contract, not just the current account's emphases. Separate the requested outcome from predictions and grading criteria; when feasible, keep test prompts separate from construction. State whether the attempt may use the retained account alone, navigate to sources, or retrieve them directly. Record actual access. A self-check is useful diagnosis, not independent evaluation.

## Answer from delivered meaning

For each requested part, make a short mapping from question to current delivered assertion(s), exact revision/selection and supporting source or declared premises. Draft the answer with those selections attached. Check the actual wording, not a heading, quotation alone, unselected nearby assertion or remembered content. Preserve attribution, conditions, units, time/population scope, comparison direction, exceptions and unknowns that delimit the answer.

For a changed or hypothetical case, write its inputs from the question alone: what is stated, omitted, changed and held constant. Keep original case facts and each hypothetical separate. Transfer only supported relationships and carry their conditions; do not carry example-specific observations, outcomes or causes into the new case. If a required case fact is unknown, retain it as unknown or answer conditionally, not as false, absent or supplied. For numerical outputs, identify the quantity/unit separately from time, index or threshold; bind inputs to the applicable relation, calculate, and compare with other outputs for that case.

For questions about events, distinguish an event from its documentation. “Not recorded” can answer that a record was not found; it cannot establish that an event did not occur. An affirmative event claim requires an occurrence observation or warranted derivation. A negative event claim needs explicit nonoccurrence evidence or a source-supported exhaustive condition; otherwise say unresolved. An incomplete closed corpus limits what can be known, not what happened.

## Diagnose and improve without rewriting history

Compare the answer to both requested parts and delivered meaning. Locate a failure: source meaning missing in the retained account, retained but not delivered, delivered but misread, unsupported inference during application, or meaning omitted while composing the answer. A correct assertion that the reader never received is a delivery issue; repairing storage alone does not fix answer composition. Preserve the initial attempt and judgment. Retrieval, revision and retest are later events and do not retroactively improve it.

When a consequential interpretation, support or scope gap warrants review, give a separate reviewer the question, draft and exact selections plus necessary source context, but not construction reasoning or a grading key. Ask for both missing requested meaning and unsupported additions. Bound the review to the gap and inspect revised parts; do not require a reviewer for every ordinary answer. Incorporate, contest with evidence, or retain findings unresolved.

Return exact questions/tasks, answers, evidence selections, access conditions, diagnosis and unresolved limits. Assess source fidelity, explanatory connections, coverage, distinctions, transfer and limits separately. A local success, favorable grade, complete reading or confident wording is not exhaustive comprehension. A writer cannot substitute a self-check for a separately required checker; report unavailable independent review as unmet rather than implying it occurred.
